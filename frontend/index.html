About

THE 42 POST is an open participatory AI research project. We welcome everyone—especially non-technologists—to forge unique intuitions, aesthetics, and wisdom into structured AI-learnable skills and test them in the Playground. We believe Semantic Capital is humanity's most precious asset, collectively guarded and maintained by global communities to preserve cultural diversity.

LEARN MORE ABOUT US →

THE 42 POST 是一个开放的参与式 AI 研究项目。我们欢迎所有人（尤其非技术人员）把独特的直觉、审美和智慧，铸造成 AI 可学习的结构化技能，并在 Playground 中测试。我们相信，语义资本是人类最珍贵的资产，应由全球社群共同守护，共同维护文化多样性。

How It Works

THE 42 POST guides you through three stages: explore, forge, and experiment.

01 Find Resonance (Skills Archive)

Browse community skills. Learn how others define values, intuition, and wisdom.

02 Forge Your Skill (Skill Forge)

IDEA → Share your thought | GENERATE → Explore deeper | FORGE → Perfect it | PUBLISH → Get Soul-Hash

03 Test & Experiment (Playground)

Test your skill in real scenarios. See how AI applies your wisdom.
